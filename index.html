TNT ALIEN RUMBLE

EPISODE LXIV: THE OLD MAN'S VENGEANCE

It is a period of interplanetary humiliation.

Thirty-nine Earth years ago, elite Gray Alien operative GLEEP-GLORP disguised himself as a frail, innocent old man with a wooden walking stick to observe Melbourne's street culture.

Out of nowhere, a pumped-up meathead with a legendary mullet and sunglasses named DUKE DAVIS strutted down the sidewalk and mercilessly BOPPED him into a trash can for no reason!

For decades, Gleep-Glorp stewed in cosmic fury aboard his mothership in Sector 7, mastering forbidden alien martial arts: the Stretchy Headbutt, the Bull Ram, the Flying Belly Flop, and the deadly Ear Twist.

Now, Gleep-Glorp has returned to Earth. He will bop through punks, dodge angry handbag-swinging grannies, trip hyperactive poodles, and give Duke the extraterrestrial thrashing of a lifetime before escaping in his flying saucer!

ENEMY FIELD ROSTER & COUNTERS

THE TRENCH-COAT DWARF
STAGE 1 ONWARD · NOT A FIGHTER — he lobs you something
Straight out of the 1987 original. Cyan hatband, khaki coat, half your height — do not hit him. Stages 1–2 he tosses a heart (+25 HP). From stage 3 he increasingly tosses a live bomb: stand over it and press [L] Low Shin Grab to defuse for 750 pts + a heart. Let it cook and it flattens everyone in the blast, you included.
Fun fact: nobody has ever seen what else is in that coat, and nobody has asked.
1. MOHAWK PUNK (SPIKE)
STAGE 1 · Knife Stabs & Flying Dropkicks
Fast street thug, 35 HP, all mouth and elbows. Intercept his flying dropkick with [K] Stretchy Headbutt, or duck it and punish on the landing.
Fun fact: reckons the mohawk is structural. It is half a can of hairspray and pure spite. Banned from every servo on the strip.
2. AXEL THE ROLLER-SKATER
STAGE 1 · High-Speed Slalom & 360° Spin Kick
Agile drive-by skater, 38 HP, quick as. Time your [K] Stretchy Headbutt to knock him clean off the skates, or take his legs with [L] Low Shin Grab.
Fun fact: has not taken the sunnies off since 1987, indoors or otherwise. One tape in the boombox and it has been chewed since Tuesday.
3. ATTACK POODLE (BARNABY)
STAGE 1 · Sonic Bark Stun & Ankle Latch Bite
Rapid low-profile rusher, 22 HP but the fastest thing on four legs here. Jump him with [W] or flatten him with [L] Shin Grab. Do not let him latch on.
Fun fact: groomed within an inch of his life and absolutely filthy about it. Goes off like a frog in a sock.
4. HANDBAG GRANNY (AGNES)
STAGE 1 · Purse Boomerang, Umbrella Shield, Helicopter Escape
45 HP. Do not strike during her Umbrella Shield — it deflects everything frontal for 0 damage. Duck under purse throws with [L] Low Trip. If she starts the rotors, she is off and you have lost the points.
Fun fact: there is a brick in that handbag. Everyone knows. Nobody is game to say it. She would argue with a signpost and win.
5. BRUTUS THE BOUNCER
STAGE 1 · Bulldozer Charge & 360° Airplane Spin
Armoured bruiser, 90 HP — the toughest thing before the boss. Jump his charge tackle with [W], then soften him with [W+J] Belly Flop. Get grabbed and you are going for a ride.
Fun fact: built like a brick dunny. Knocked back your ID in 1987 and would do it again tomorrow.
6. THE MOTORCYCLIST
STAGE 1 ONWARD · Revs, then a flat-out drive-by pass
A stage boss in the 1987 original, restored. He never brawls — he revs on the spot, then charges at speed 5.6, the quickest thing in the game. Light jabs CLANG off the bike mid-charge; you need airborne or heavy ([W+K] Dropkick, [W+J] Belly Flop, [U] Bull Ram). Or just jump [W] and he goes underneath. Fair game between passes.
Fun fact: the bike is louder than it is fast, which is saying something. Has never once indicated.
7. HOOPSTER (HOOPS #23)
STAGE 2 · Active Dribble, Bouncing Ball, Monster Dunk
Towering 84px giant with a 140px reach — he can hit you from the next postcode. Jump the bouncing balls with [W], close the gap, and bowl him over with [U] Bull Ram.
Fun fact: four metres of him and he will tell you, unprompted, that he was robbed at the draft.
8. BRUNO THE STRONGMAN
STAGE 2 · 100KG Barbell Whirlwind
75 HP and invulnerable during the 360° whirlwind — everything bounces except a trip. Keep your distance, wait it out, then come in behind with [O] Donkey Kick.
Fun fact: it is genuinely 100 kilos. He will let you know. Repeatedly. Unprompted. At length.
9. DUKE DAVIS (FINAL BOSS)
STAGE 4 · Heavy Haymakers, Dragon Kicks & Enraged Aura
The legendary 1987 mullet vigilante, and the bloke who started all this by belting a harmless old man into a bin. Dodge his haymakers vertically, soften him with combos, then finish it with the [SPACE] UFO Tractor Beam.
Fun fact: the mullet is load-bearing. Beat him and he retires from street brawling to move to Gosford, which he insists was the plan all along.
